⌨️ LEARN TOUCH TYPING — BEGINNER TO ADVANCED

Touch typing is the skill of typing without looking at the keyboard, using all 10 fingers with each finger assigned to specific keys. It is the single most effective way to reach high WPM speeds. Our interactive keymap visualization shows which hand and finger presses each key in real time as you type.

Start with home row practice — place your left fingers on A S D F and right fingers on J K L ; and never move them far. This finger placement technique is the foundation of all fast typists. Our Easy mode is specifically designed as a beginner typing course that uses simple, common words so new learners can build muscle memory without frustration.

Once comfortable, advance to Medium and Hard difficulty for advanced typing drills with punctuation, numbers, and complex sentences — the same kind of text used in professional typing assessments and data entry typing tests.

🎯 HOW TO IMPROVE YOUR TYPING SPEED

1. Practice daily — Even 10–15 minutes of typing exercises per day produces measurable WPM gains within weeks.
2. Accuracy first — Don't rush. Fix your error rate below 5% before pushing for higher speed.
3. Use all 10 fingers — Learn touch typing with proper finger placement using the home row method.
4. Don't look at the keyboard — Force yourself to look only at the screen. Use our keymap for visual guidance instead.
5. Progress difficulty — Start on Easy for beginner typing drills, move to Hard for advanced typing training.
6. Track and compete — Save your scores and use the global leaderboard as motivation for your typing challenge.

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the best free typing test online?
Rocket Typing offers a completely free online typing test with real-time WPM, accuracy, a global leaderboard, and difficulty modes — no login needed. It is one of the most accurate and feature-rich free typing tests available.
How do I take a 1-minute typing test?
Select "Time" mode on Rocket Typing and choose 60 seconds. Start typing the highlighted text and your WPM and accuracy will be calculated automatically when the timer ends.
Is there a typing test for kids?
Yes! Rocket Typing's Easy mode uses simple common words making it perfect for students and kids learning keyboarding skills. The visual keymap helps children understand proper finger placement without frustration.
Can I use this as a typing certification?
Rocket Typing provides detailed WPM, accuracy, and raw WPM scores that can serve as a typing benchmark. For a professional typing assessment or formal certification, complete a 5-minute test on Hard mode and screenshot your results.
What typing speed do I need for data entry jobs?
Most data entry and transcription typing jobs require 50–60 WPM with 95%+ accuracy. Practice on Rocket Typing's Hard mode to build up to professional typing speed for data entry assessments.
How is WPM calculated?
WPM is calculated as (correctly typed characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes elapsed. The standard word length of 5 characters is used so results are consistent regardless of word length. Raw WPM counts all keystrokes including errors.
