Tim Cook Stepping Down as Apple CEO

📅 Trending on September 1, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read 🏷 News / Business

On September 1, 2026, a major change takes place at Apple. Tim Cook is stepping down as CEO after leading the company for 15 years. John Ternus, the longtime head of Apple’s hardware engineering, becomes the new chief executive officer.

Cook is not leaving Apple completely. He moves into the role of executive chairman. In this position, he will stay involved in key areas, including relationships with governments and policymakers around the world.

Cook’s Time as CEO

Tim Cook took over as CEO in 2011 after Steve Jobs. Under his leadership, Apple grew into one of the most valuable companies in the world. The company expanded its product line with items like the Apple Watch and AirPods. Services such as Apple Music, Apple TV+, and the App Store also became much larger businesses.

Apple’s value rose sharply during his years in charge. The company reached major milestones and built a strong global presence.

Who Is John Ternus?

John Ternus has worked at Apple for about 25 years. He played a big role in developing many of the company’s most important products, including the iPhone, iPad, Mac, and AirPods. As the new CEO, he will lead day-to-day operations and guide Apple’s next chapter.

Apple has planned this transition carefully. Cook worked closely with Ternus over the summer to make the handover smooth.

What Comes Next

The change comes just before Apple’s next big product event. Fans and the tech world will watch closely to see how the company moves forward under new leadership while still keeping Cook’s experience close by.

This marks the end of one era at Apple and the start of another.
