McDonald’s India X Account Controversy: What Happened

📅 Trending on September 6, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read 🏷 Viral / Internet Culture

On September 6, 2026, unusual posts appeared on McDonald’s India’s official X account. The messages claimed to come from an intern who said they had not been paid for months.

The posts named a person called Amit Joshi as the boss and said the unpaid amount was around Rs 60,000. The writer claimed they had to take extra jobs in customer support and delivery. Some messages also talked about money problems linked to trading memecoins and asked for community support.

The posts were deleted soon after they appeared. Screenshots spread quickly online and started wide discussion.

How People Reacted

Many users wondered how the messages got onto the company’s verified account. Some thought a real intern had access and used it to speak out. Others believed the account was hacked or taken over, possibly as part of a scam, especially because later posts mentioned cryptocurrency wallets and memecoins.

The claims have not been independently confirmed. There is no clear public proof that the person worked as an intern at McDonald’s India or that the named person withheld pay.

Company Response

McDonald’s India responded on the same account. They shared a light post with an image and the message that their real posts rarely go this viral. They used hashtags linked to fake news and memecoins, suggesting the earlier messages were not official.

Why It Drew Attention

The story mixed workplace issues, social media security, and online trends. It raised questions about how company accounts are managed and protected. It also showed how quickly screenshots can travel even after posts are removed.

At this time, the full details remain unclear. The posts are gone, the company has distanced itself from them, and the identity of the poster is not verified. The incident remains a short but widely discussed moment on social media.
