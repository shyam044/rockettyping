Trump Says He Is in “Deciding Mode” on Iran and Warns of “Very Big Things” as Tehran Issues Seven Conditions

📅 Trending on September 21, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read 🏷 News / World

On September 20, 2026, US President Donald Trump said he is in “deciding mode” about the ongoing conflict with Iran. He warned that “very big things” could happen soon if Iran does not change its approach.

Trump spoke about three possible paths. These include strong military action, continued economic pressure, or reaching a deal. He also said Iranian leaders need to behave and that major decisions are coming in the near future.

Iran’s Seven Conditions

At the same time, Iranian officials said they sent seven conditions to the United States through Qatari mediators. These conditions are meant to start talks and end the war.

Key points from Iran include:

  • An end to fighting on all fronts
  • Release of frozen Iranian funds
  • An end to the US naval blockade
  • Other steps related to ending interference and reopening the Strait of Hormuz

Iranian officials said they are ready for talks if the conditions are met. They also warned they are prepared for further action if the demands are rejected.

Possible Next Steps

Trump said he would probably be open to meeting Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian during the United Nations General Assembly meetings in New York this week.

The conflict has lasted several months. Both sides continue to discuss ways forward while tensions remain high in the region.

World leaders and markets are watching closely for any new developments in the coming days.
