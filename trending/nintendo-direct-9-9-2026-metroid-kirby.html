Nintendo Direct 9.9.2026: All the Big News in Simple Words

📅 Trending on September 9, 2026 ⏱ 4 min read 🏷 Gaming

Nintendo held a new Nintendo Direct on September 9, 2026. The show lasted about 45 minutes. It focused on games coming to the Nintendo Switch 2 this winter and beyond.

This Direct came right after a special Zelda 40th Anniversary Direct the day before. Fans got lots of new information in a short time.

What Was the Focus?

Nintendo talked mostly about Switch 2 games launching soon. There were new first-party games, updated older titles, and many third-party ports and new releases. The show mixed big reveals with free updates and release dates.

Biggest First-Party Reveals

Two big new Nintendo games stood out.

Metroid Ravenous is a new 2D side-scrolling adventure starring Samus. It feels like a follow-up to Metroid Dread. The trailer showed Samus fighting tough enemies and using a new energy system where she can “eat” foes to gain power. The tagline is “eat or be eaten.” It launches on January 28, 2027, only on Switch 2. Special edition and amiibo figures were also shown.

Kirby and the World Beyond is a new 3D Kirby game. Kirby can freely explore a big open world and use his classic copy abilities in creative ways. The trailer ends with a cool reveal of a larger world beyond. It comes out in spring 2027 to celebrate Kirby’s 35th anniversary.

Other first-party news included:

  • Pikmin 4 Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy (November 12, 2026). It adds voice commands and new challenge modes.
  • Free update for Mario Kart World with classic tracks (available the same day).
  • Free multiplayer update for Star Fox with four-player split-screen (later date).
  • Star Fox Adventures added to Nintendo Switch Online GameCube library (available the same day).
  • Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity – Definitive Edition (February 25, 2027).
  • Professor Layton and the New World of Steam (December 10, 2026).
  • Yo-kai Watch 2: Haunted Domain is in development (no date yet).

Popular Third-Party Games

Many other games got Switch 2 news:

  • Monster Hunter Wilds arrives December 4, 2026, with local play support and a future big expansion.
  • Final Fantasy VII Revelation (the end of the remake series) comes April 8, 2027.
  • Crisis Core –Final Fantasy VII– Reunion launched the same day.
  • Resident Evil 2, 3, and 4 remakes come to Switch 2 on October 16, 2026.
  • The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered arrives September 29, 2026.
  • Persona 4 Revival launches May 20, 2027. Persona 6 is also confirmed for Switch 2 (no date yet).
  • Xenoblade Chronicles 3 Nintendo Switch 2 Edition arrives December 3, 2026.
  • Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave launches September 17, 2026.
  • Other titles like Kingdom Come: Deliverance II, Stage Fright (from the Overcooked team), Cairn, Mega Man: Dual Override, and more got dates or confirms.

Nintendo also showed free updates, smaller indie games, and ports of popular titles.

How Fans Reacted

Many people liked the new Metroid and Kirby games the most. Others were happy to see strong third-party support for Switch 2. Some wanted more brand-new Mario or bigger surprises, but the Direct still gave a solid list of winter and 2027 games.

Where to Watch

You can still watch the full Nintendo Direct 9.9.2026 on Nintendo’s official YouTube channel. After the Direct, Nintendo also ran a Treehouse Live stream with extra gameplay of some titles.

This Direct showed that Switch 2 has a busy road ahead. From big new adventures like Metroid Ravenous and Kirby and the World Beyond to familiar favorites getting better versions, there is plenty for fans to look forward to in the coming months.

What was your favorite announcement? The new Metroid, the open-world Kirby, or one of the big third-party ports?
