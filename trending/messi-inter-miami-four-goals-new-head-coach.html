Messi & Inter Miami: The Latest Global Football Buzz

📅 Trending on August 31, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read 🏷 Sports

Lionel Messi keeps making headlines in 2026. The 39-year-old star continues to light up Major League Soccer with Inter Miami, and the latest news has fans around the world talking.

On August 29, Messi delivered one of his best performances of the season. He scored four goals and added an assist as Inter Miami beat CF Montreal 7-1. The big win ended a five-game stretch without a victory for the team. Messi now leads the MLS scoring race with 18 goals this season.

His goals came in different ways. He scored from a free kick, showed his skill with a solo run, added a clever chip, and finished with another free kick late in the game. Teammates and coaches praised the display. Interim coach Guillermo Hoyos called it pure football beauty and said words fall short when describing Messi’s play.

Coaching Change at Inter Miami

Around the same time, Inter Miami announced a new head coach. Cristian “Kily” González took over the role. He is a former Argentina player who once shared the field with Messi. The club hopes the new coach will bring fresh energy as the team pushes for a strong finish in the MLS season.

Why the World Keeps Watching

Messi still draws huge attention. Even at 39, he creates magic on the pitch. Fans love watching him play for Inter Miami after his long career in Europe and his success with Argentina. His recent form shows he remains one of the top players in the league.

Inter Miami sits near the top of the Eastern Conference standings. With Messi leading the attack and a new coach in place, the team looks ready for the final stretch of the season.

Football fans across the globe continue to follow every match. Messi’s ability to produce special moments keeps the buzz alive and makes Inter Miami one of the most watched teams in MLS.
