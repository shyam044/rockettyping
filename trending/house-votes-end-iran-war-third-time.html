US House Votes to End Iran War for Third Time

📅 Trending on September 16, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read 🏷 News / Politics

On September 15, 2026, the US House of Representatives voted to pass a war powers resolution that would stop further US military action in Iran without Congress approval. The final tally was 220 to 204.

All voting Democrats supported the measure. Seven Republicans also voted yes. This is the third time the House has approved a similar resolution this year.

What the Vote Means

The resolution directs the president to remove US forces from hostilities with Iran. It allows troops to stay only if needed to defend American or allied forces and bases.

Supporters say Congress, not the president alone, should decide on wars. They point out that the conflict has lasted longer than first expected and costs a lot of money.

Republican Rep. Zach Nunn of Iowa, who voted yes for the first time, said sustained combat now needs congressional approval. He added that he will not support another open-ended war.

The Seven Republicans

The seven Republicans who joined Democrats include:

  • Thomas Massie of Kentucky
  • Tom Barrett of Michigan
  • Warren Davidson of Ohio
  • Brian Fitzpatrick of Pennsylvania
  • Nancy Mace of South Carolina
  • Mariannette Miller-Meeks of Iowa
  • Zach Nunn of Iowa

Three of them voted this way for the first time.

What Happens Next

None of the earlier House resolutions reached the president’s desk. President Trump is expected to veto this one if it advances.

The Senate has not yet acted on previous versions. It is unclear if this latest measure will move forward before the November midterm elections.

The war began in late February 2026. It has now lasted about seven months. Lawmakers on both sides continue to debate costs, strategy, and the role of Congress in military decisions.

This vote shows growing concern in the House about the ongoing conflict. It remains a symbolic step until both chambers of Congress and the president act.
