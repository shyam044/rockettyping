Alexander Zverev Wins the 2026 US Open Men’s Title

📅 Trending on September 13, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read 🏷 Sports

German tennis star Alexander Zverev is the 2026 US Open men’s singles champion. On Sunday, September 13, 2026, he beat American Ben Shelton in the final at Arthur Ashe Stadium in New York.

The final score was 6-3, 7-6(2), 5-7, 6-2. It took just over three and a half hours. Zverev, the world No. 2, showed strong serving and steady play to claim his first US Open title.

A Big Year for Zverev

This win is Zverev’s second Grand Slam title of 2026. He already won the French Open earlier in the year. At 29 years old, he has now won two of the four biggest tennis tournaments in the same season.

He told his team after the match that Germany had gone 30 years without a men’s Grand Slam title, and now they have two in just a few months. Zverev also reached the Wimbledon final this year, making it a standout season for him.

The Match Against Shelton

Shelton was playing in his first Grand Slam final. The New York crowd strongly supported the American player, hoping for the first US men’s major title since Andy Roddick won in 2003.

Zverev took the first two sets. Shelton fought back to win the third set. But Zverev stayed calm, broke Shelton’s serve in the fourth set, and closed out the match. At one point, Zverev was so focused that he did not immediately realize he had won.

Shelton played well but could not overcome Zverev’s experience and powerful game.

What It Means

Zverev becomes the first German man to win the US Open since Boris Becker in 1989. The victory also ends any chance of an American man winning a major title in 2026.

Zverev earned $5.5 million in prize money for the win. He stays near the top of the world rankings and has put together one of the best years of his career.

Tennis fans around the world are talking about Zverev’s breakthrough. After years of coming close, he has now collected two major trophies in 2026. Many are calling it a career-defining season for the tall German player.

The 2026 US Open will be remembered for Zverev’s strong performance and for Shelton’s impressive run to the final as a rising American star.
