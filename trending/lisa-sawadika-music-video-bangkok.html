LISA’s “SaWaDiKa” Official Music Video: A Bold Hello from Thailand

📅 Trending on September 7, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read 🏷 Entertainment / Music

LISA, the Thai star from BLACKPINK, dropped her new single “SaWaDiKa” in early September 2026. The official music video came with it and quickly became one of the biggest talking points in music right now.

The title “SaWaDiKa” comes from the Thai greeting “sawatdee ka,” which means “hello.” LISA uses it as a proud nod to her roots while showing the world her sharp rap style and strong presence.

About the Song

“SaWaDiKa” is the lead track from LISA’s upcoming EP called Press Play, set to arrive on October 23, 2026. She released the song through her company LLOUD and RCA Records.

The track is pure hip-hop energy. LISA returns to her rap roots with clear, confident delivery over a heavy beat. She co-wrote the song, and the production keeps it fresh and powerful. Fans already call it a strong statement of who she is — a global star who never forgets where she comes from.

The Music Video

Director Bang Jae Yeob (known for many popular K-pop videos) filmed the clip entirely in Bangkok, Thailand. The video takes viewers through real city spots, including areas near Hua Lamphong railway station and other local streets and spaces.

LISA appears in several striking looks. One standout is a red cut-out leather outfit paired with boxing shorts that display “SaWaDiKa” in shiny letters — a clear nod to Muay Thai. Other scenes show her in bold fashion pieces, moving with a crew of dancers through neon streets, rooftops, and urban settings.

The video mixes high fashion with Thai culture. Viewers see tuk-tuks, local street energy, and details that celebrate Thailand. It feels like a love letter to her home while looking modern and global at the same time.

Record-Breaking Success

The music video made history fast. It pulled in about 70.8 million views in its first 24 hours. That made it the biggest YouTube music video debut of 2026 so far and the second-highest opening day ever for a solo artist (behind LISA’s own earlier track “Lalisa”).

Within roughly two days, the video crossed 100 million views. Fans around the world streamed it nonstop, pushing it to the top of music charts and trends. The strong numbers show how much people connect with LISA’s mix of confidence, culture, and catchy energy.

Why It Matters

“SaWaDiKa” stands out because it feels personal. LISA puts Thai identity front and center — the greeting, the locations, the cultural touches — while delivering the kind of sharp, stylish performance her fans expect. It is not just another release. It is a clear “hello” to a new chapter that stays true to her roots.

The video and song have already sparked big excitement for the full Press Play EP later this year. Many fans say this is LISA at her most confident and proud.

If you have not watched the official music video yet, it is easy to find on YouTube. “SaWaDiKa” is a short, high-energy watch that mixes strong rap, bold fashion, and Thai pride in one package. LISA is saying hello in her own unique way — and the world is answering loud and clear.
