FIBA Women’s Basketball World Cup 2026: Action Underway in Berlin

📅 Trending on September 5, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read 🏷 Sports

The biggest event in women’s international basketball is taking place right now. The FIBA Women’s Basketball World Cup 2026 is being held in Berlin, Germany, from September 4 to 13.

Sixteen teams from around the world are competing for the title. The United States enters as the strong favorite. The Americans are looking for a fifth straight World Cup win. Other top teams include Australia, Spain, France, China, and Japan.

Early Results and Highlights

On the opening day, Team USA started strong with a clear win over China. Young star Caitlin Clark made a big impression in her World Cup debut. She scored points and set up many plays for her teammates.

Japan also had a strong start, winning a close game against Mali. Australia defeated Puerto Rico, and Spain beat host nation Germany. More games continue on September 5 and throughout the group stage.

How the Tournament Works

The 16 teams are split into four groups of four. Each team plays the others in its group. The top teams advance toward the quarterfinals, semifinals, and final.

Group winners move straight to the quarterfinals. Second- and third-place teams play extra games to earn a spot. The final is set for September 13.

Why It Matters

This tournament is the top stage for women’s basketball between Olympic Games. Fans get to see the best players from the WNBA and leagues worldwide. Stars like Breanna Stewart, Paige Bueckers, and players from many countries are on show.

Games are being played in front of big crowds in Berlin. Interest in women’s basketball keeps growing, and this World Cup is drawing attention from fans across the globe.

The competition is just getting started. More exciting matchups are coming in the days ahead as teams battle for the championship.
