Apple “Surprise and Shine” Event: iPhone Duo and All the New Products Explained Simply

📅 Trending on September 10, 2026 ⏱ 4 min read 🏷 Technology

Apple held its big “Surprise and Shine” event on September 9, 2026. New CEO John Ternus led the show. The main star was the company’s first foldable iPhone, called the iPhone Duo. Apple also showed the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max, Apple Watch Series 12, Apple Watch Ultra 4, and AirPods 5.

Here is everything in easy words.

iPhone Duo – Apple’s First Foldable Phone

The iPhone Duo is a book-style foldable phone. When closed, it is about the size of a passport and has a 5.4-inch outer screen. When you open it, you get a large 7.6-inch inner screen – the biggest screen Apple has ever put on an iPhone.

Both screens look sharp and bright. Apple says the crease is almost invisible thanks to a special coating. The phone runs on the new A20 Pro chip. It has dual batteries for long use – up to 44 hours of video on the outer screen and around 31 hours on the inner one.

You can use two apps at the same time on the big screen. Later this year it will also support the Apple Pencil. It comes in Star White and Night Sky colors.

Price starts at $1,999 for 256 GB of storage. Higher storage options go up to 2 TB. Pre-orders start October 16, and it goes on sale October 23.

iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max

These are the new top regular iPhones. They use the same powerful A20 Pro chip. The main camera now has a variable aperture, which means better photos in different light. Battery life is the best Apple has offered so far.

Colors include Black, Silver, Glacier, and Burgundy.

  • iPhone 18 Pro starts at $1,199
  • iPhone 18 Pro Max starts at $1,299

Pre-orders begin September 12. They arrive in stores on September 18. Note: The regular (non-Pro) iPhone 18 is expected next year.

Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4

The Series 12 gets better health tracking. It checks heart rate more often and gives a daily readiness score to show how ready your body is for exercise. Ceramic case options are back.

The Ultra 4 is the tough outdoor version with longer battery life (up to 50 hours in some cases).

  • Series 12 starts at $399
  • Ultra 4 starts at $799

Both are available for pre-order now and go on sale September 18.

AirPods 5

The new standard AirPods finally get active noise cancellation. Sound quality is better, and they work well with the new Siri AI features.

Price starts at $129 (or $149 with the wireless charging case). Pre-orders are open, and they launch September 18.

Other Notes

Apple also talked about better Siri with AI features that start rolling out with the new software updates around September 14. The event focused on these main hardware products.

The iPhone Duo is the biggest change. It is expensive and aimed at people who want something new and different. The Pro iPhones, watches, and AirPods offer solid upgrades for everyday users.

You can still watch the full event on Apple’s website or YouTube if you missed it. Pre-orders for most products open soon, so check Apple’s site for exact times in your country.

Which new product interests you most – the foldable iPhone Duo, the Pro phones, the watches, or the AirPods?
