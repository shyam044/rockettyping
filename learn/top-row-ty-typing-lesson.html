🖐️ Practice Typing TY

T is typed with your left index finger and Y is typed with your right index finger. These two keys are next to each other on the top row. These exercises help you build coordination and muscle memory while typing T and Y without looking at the keyboard.

How to Type T and Y Correctly

The letters T and Y are important top-row keys used in touch typing. Press T with your left index finger and Y with your right index finger.

Keep both hands relaxed and return your index fingers to their home-row positions after each keystroke. Practice reaching upward to T and Y without looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type T and Y?

The letter T is typed with your left index finger. The letter Y is typed with your right index finger. Return both fingers to their home-row positions after each keystroke.

Why practice T and Y together?

Practicing T and Y together teaches your left and right index fingers to coordinate independently while reaching from the home row to the top row.
