🖐️ Practice Typing AS

A is typed with your left pinky finger and S is typed with your left ring finger. These two keys are next to each other on the home row. These exercises help you build coordination and muscle memory while typing A and S without looking at the keyboard.

How to Type A and S Correctly

The letters A and S are two of the main left-hand home-row keys used in touch typing. Press A with your left pinky and S with your left ring finger.

Keep your left hand relaxed and positioned on the home row. Your pinky should rest naturally on A while your ring finger rests on S. Practice moving between the two keys without looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type A and S?

The letter A is typed with your left pinky finger. The letter S is typed with your left ring finger. Both fingers should return to their home-row positions after each keystroke.

Why practice A and S together?

Practicing A and S together teaches your left pinky and ring finger to move independently. It also builds the coordination needed for longer home-row typing exercises.
