🖐️ Type the Letter P

P is typed with your right pinky finger. Reach up from the ; key to press P, then return your finger to its home-row position. These 4 exercises build the muscle memory you need to type P accurately without looking at the keyboard.

How to Type the Letter P Correctly

The letter P is a top-row key typed with your right pinky finger. From the home row, your pinky moves upward from the semicolon key to P and then returns to the semicolon key.

This lesson uses four short drills — a single tap, a double tap, a triple tap, and a building rhythm from one to five letters — so your right pinky learns the movement needed to reach P accurately and consistently.

Which finger types the letter P?

Your right pinky finger. Start with your pinky resting on the semicolon key, move it upward to P, press the key, and return to the home row.

Is P a home-row key?

No. P is on the top row of a QWERTY keyboard. Your right pinky reaches upward from the semicolon key to type it.
