⌨️ Type U I O P

U,I,O and P are top-row keys. Keep your hands anchored on the home row and reach upward with the correct fingers. These 4 exercises help build accuracy, coordination and muscle memory for the top row.

How to Type U I O P Correctly

The letters U, I, O and P are located on the top row of a QWERTY keyboard. Keep your hands positioned on the home row and reach upward to press each key.

This lesson contains four progressive drills. Start with individual letters, then practice two-letter combinations, three-letter sequences, and finally longer U-I-O-P patterns.

Which fingers type U, I, O and P?

On a standard QWERTY keyboard, U is typed with the right index finger, I with the right middle finger, O with the right ring finger, and P with the right pinky finger.

Are U, I, O and P home-row keys?

No. U, I, O and P are top-row keys. Your fingers reach upward from their home-row positions while your hands remain relaxed and anchored.
