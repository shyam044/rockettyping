How to Type L and Semicolon Correctly

The letter L and semicolon key ; are two important right-hand home-row keys used in touch typing. Press L with your right ring finger and ; with your right pinky finger.

Keep your right hand relaxed and positioned on the home row. Your ring finger should rest naturally on L while your pinky rests on the semicolon key. Practice moving between the two keys without looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type L and semicolon?

The letter L is typed with your right ring finger. The semicolon key is typed with your right pinky finger. Both fingers should return to their home-row positions after each keystroke.

Why practice L and semicolon together?

Practicing L and semicolon together teaches your right ring and pinky fingers to move independently. It also builds the coordination needed for longer home-row typing exercises.
