🖐️ Practice Typing ZX

Z is typed with your left pinky finger and X is typed with your left ring finger. These two keys are next to each other on the bottom row. These exercises help you build coordination and muscle memory while typing Z and X without looking at the keyboard.

How to Type Z and X Correctly

The letters Z and X are important left-hand bottom-row keys used in touch typing. Press Z with your left pinky finger and X with your left ring finger.

Keep your left hand relaxed and return your fingers to their home-row positions after each keystroke. Practice reaching downward to Z and X without looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type Z and X?

The letter Z is typed with your left pinky finger. The letter X is typed with your left ring finger. Return both fingers to their home-row positions after each keystroke.

Why practice Z and X together?

Practicing Z and X together teaches your left pinky and ring fingers to move independently while reaching from the home row to the bottom row.
