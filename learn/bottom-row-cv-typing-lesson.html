🖐️ Practice Typing CV

C is typed with your left middle finger and V is typed with your left index finger. These two keys are next to each other on the bottom row. These exercises help you build coordination and muscle memory while typing C and V without looking at the keyboard.

How to Type C and V Correctly

The letters C and V are important left-hand bottom-row keys used in touch typing. Press C with your left middle finger and V with your left index finger.

Keep your left hand relaxed and return your fingers to their home-row positions after each keystroke. Practice reaching downward to C and V without looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type C and V?

The letter C is typed with your left middle finger. The letter V is typed with your left index finger. Return both fingers to their home-row positions after each keystroke.

Why practice C and V together?

Practicing C and V together teaches your left middle and index fingers to move independently while reaching from the home row to the bottom row.
