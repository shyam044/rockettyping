🔢 Lesson: Type Numbers 5 and 6

5 is typed with your left index finger, while 6 is typed with your right index finger. These 4 exercises combine both number-row keys to build accurate typing muscle memory.

How to Type Numbers 5 and 6 Correctly

The number 5 is typed with your left index finger, while 6 is typed with your right index finger. Keep both hands positioned on the home row and reach upward with the correct finger for each number.

These four progressive exercises help you switch accurately between 5 and 6 while developing number-row muscle memory. Focus on accuracy first and avoid looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type the numbers 5 and 6?

Your left index finger types 5, and your right index finger types 6. Return your fingers toward the home row after each keystroke.

How should I practice numbers 5 and 6?

Keep both hands relaxed on the home row. Reach upward for 5 with your left index finger and 6 with your right index finger, focusing on clean and accurate keystrokes.
