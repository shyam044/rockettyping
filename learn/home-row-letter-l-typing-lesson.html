🖐️ Lesson: Type the Letter L

L is typed with your right ring finger and is one of the four main right-hand home-row anchor keys. It sits directly under your right ring finger, next to K. These 4 exercises build your muscle memory from a single tap all the way up to a five-letter run. Keep your eyes on the text above, not on your hands.

How to Type the Letter L Correctly

The letter L is one of the main right-hand home-row keys used by touch typists. It is pressed with your right ring finger. Keep your other right-hand fingers resting naturally on J, K, and ; while your ring finger presses L.

This lesson uses four short drills — a single tap, a double tap, a triple tap, and a building rhythm from one to five letters — so your right ring finger learns the exact position and movement needed to hit L accurately and consistently without looking at the keyboard.

Which finger types the letter L?

Your right ring finger. When your right hand is correctly positioned on the home row, your ring finger should rest naturally on L. Press L with a small, controlled downward tap while keeping your other fingers relaxed.

Why learn L on the home row?

L is one of the main right-hand home-row keys. Practicing L helps your right ring finger learn its home-row position and builds the foundation for accurate touch typing.
