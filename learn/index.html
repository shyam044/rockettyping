A Free Typing Course Built One Key at a Time

Rocket Typing's beginner typing course is 100% free and requires no sign-up. Instead of dropping you into a giant curriculum on day one, each lesson lives on its own page and isolates a single key or skill — so you can search for exactly what you want to practice, whether that's a specific letter, the home row, or full sentences.

Lesson 1 starts with the letter A, one of the four home row anchor keys your left hand always returns to. Every lesson pairs a short set of exercises with a live animated keyboard and virtual hands, so you always know which finger to use without ever looking down at your keyboard.
