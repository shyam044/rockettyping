🖐️ Practice Typing QW

Q is typed with your left pinky finger and W is typed with your left ring finger. These two keys are next to each other on the top row. These exercises help you build coordination and muscle memory while typing Q and W without looking at the keyboard.

How to Type Q and W Correctly

The letters Q and W are two important left-hand top-row keys used in touch typing. Press Q with your left pinky finger and W with your left ring finger.

Keep your left hand relaxed and return your fingers to their home-row positions after each keystroke. Practice reaching upward to Q and W without looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type Q and W?

The letter Q is typed with your left pinky finger. The letter W is typed with your left ring finger. Return both fingers to their home-row positions after each keystroke.

Why practice Q and W together?

Practicing Q and W together teaches your left pinky and ring fingers to move independently while reaching from the home row to the top row.
