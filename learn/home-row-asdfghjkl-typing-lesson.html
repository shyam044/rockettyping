🖐️ Home Row: ASDFGHJKL;

ASDFGHJKL; are the home row keys on a standard QWERTY keyboard. Your fingers rest on these keys as the starting position for touch typing. These 4 exercises build accuracy and muscle memory using common words made from the home row.

How to Type the Home Row ASDFGHJKL;

The home row is the foundation of touch typing. On a standard QWERTY keyboard, the letter keys are A, S, D, F, G, H, J, K, L, followed by the semicolon (;) key. Your fingers return to these positions after reaching for keys on other rows.

These four exercises start with short combinations and gradually progress to longer words. Practice without looking at the keyboard and focus on keeping each finger assigned to its correct home-row key.

What are the home row keys?

The home row contains A, S, D, F, G, H, J, K, L, and the semicolon key (;). Your fingers use these keys as their starting and returning positions.

Why should I practice the home row first?

The home row teaches the basic finger positions used in touch typing. Once your fingers can find these keys automatically, reaching for the top and bottom rows becomes much easier.
