🔢 Lesson: Type Numbers 1 to 0

Practice the complete number row from 1 through 0. These 4 exercises combine all ten number-row keys to build accurate typing muscle memory.

How to Type Numbers 1 to 0 Correctly

On a standard QWERTY keyboard, 1 is typed with your left pinky finger, 2 with your left ring finger, 3 with your left middle finger, 4 and 5 with your left index finger, 6 and 7 with your right index finger, 8 with your right middle finger, 9 with your right ring finger, and 0 with your right pinky finger.

These four progressive exercises help you move across the complete number row while developing accurate number-row muscle memory. Focus on accuracy first and avoid looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type the numbers 1 to 0?

Your left pinky types 1, left ring finger types 2, left middle finger types 3, left index finger types 4 and 5, right index finger types 6 and 7, right middle finger types 8, right ring finger types 9, and right pinky finger types 0.

How should I practice typing numbers 1 to 0?

Keep both hands relaxed on the home row. Reach upward for each number with the correct finger, focusing on clean and accurate keystrokes before trying to type faster.
