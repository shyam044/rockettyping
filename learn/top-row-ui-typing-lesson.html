🖐️ Practice Typing UI

U is typed with your right index finger and I is typed with your right middle finger. These two keys are next to each other on the top row. These exercises help you build coordination and muscle memory while typing U and I without looking at the keyboard.

How to Type U and I Correctly

The letters U and I are important right-hand top-row keys used in touch typing. Press U with your right index finger and I with your right middle finger.

Keep your right hand relaxed and return your fingers to their home-row positions after each keystroke. Practice reaching upward to U and I without looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type U and I?

The letter U is typed with your right index finger. The letter I is typed with your right middle finger. Return both fingers to their home-row positions after each keystroke.

Why practice U and I together?

Practicing U and I together teaches your right index and middle fingers to move independently while reaching from the home row to the top row.
