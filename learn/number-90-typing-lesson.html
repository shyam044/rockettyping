🔢 Lesson: Type Numbers 9 and 0

9 is typed with your right ring finger, while 0 is typed with your right pinky finger. These 4 exercises combine both number-row keys to build accurate typing muscle memory.

How to Type Numbers 9 and 0 Correctly

The number 9 is typed with your right ring finger, while 0 is typed with your right pinky finger. Keep your right hand positioned on the home row and reach upward with the correct finger for each number.

These four progressive exercises help you switch accurately between 9 and 0 while developing number-row muscle memory. Focus on accuracy first and avoid looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type the numbers 9 and 0?

Your right ring finger types 9, and your right pinky finger types 0. Return your fingers toward the home row after each keystroke.

How should I practice numbers 9 and 0?

Keep your right hand relaxed on the home row. Reach upward for 9 with your right ring finger and 0 with your right pinky finger, focusing on clean and accurate keystrokes.
