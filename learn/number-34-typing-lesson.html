🔢 Lesson: Type Numbers 3 and 4

3 is typed with your left middle finger, while 4 is typed with your left index finger. These 4 exercises combine both number-row keys to build accurate typing muscle memory.

How to Type Numbers 3 and 4 Correctly

The number 3 is typed with your left middle finger, while 4 is typed with your left index finger. Keep your left hand positioned on the home row and reach upward with the correct finger for each number.

These four progressive exercises help you switch accurately between 3 and 4 while developing number-row muscle memory. Focus on accuracy first and avoid looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type the numbers 3 and 4?

Your left middle finger types 3, and your left index finger types 4. Return your fingers toward the home row after each keystroke.

How should I practice numbers 3 and 4?

Keep your left hand relaxed on the home row. Reach upward for 3 with your middle finger and 4 with your index finger, focusing on clean and accurate keystrokes.
