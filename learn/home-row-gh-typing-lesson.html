🖐️ Practice Typing GH

G is typed with your left index finger and H is typed with your right index finger. These two keys sit next to each other near the center of the home row. These exercises help you build coordination and muscle memory while typing G and H without looking at the keyboard.

How to Type G and H Correctly

The letters G and H are important keys for touch typing. Press G with your left index finger and H with your right index finger.

Keep both hands relaxed and positioned correctly on the home row. Your left index finger moves from F toward G, while your right index finger moves from J toward H. Practice moving between G and H without looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type G and H?

The letter G is typed with your left index finger. The letter H is typed with your right index finger. Return your fingers toward their home-row positions after each keystroke.

Why practice G and H together?

Practicing G and H together helps your left and right index fingers work independently. It also builds coordination between both hands and prepares you for longer touch-typing exercises.
