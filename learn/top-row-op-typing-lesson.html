🖐️ Practice Typing OP

O is typed with your right ring finger and P is typed with your right pinky finger. These two keys are next to each other on the top row. These exercises help you build coordination and muscle memory while typing O and P without looking at the keyboard.

How to Type O and P Correctly

The letters O and P are important right-hand top-row keys used in touch typing. Press O with your right ring finger and P with your right pinky finger.

Keep your right hand relaxed and return your fingers to their home-row positions after each keystroke. Practice reaching upward to O and P without looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type O and P?

The letter O is typed with your right ring finger. The letter P is typed with your right pinky finger. Return both fingers to their home-row positions after each keystroke.

Why practice O and P together?

Practicing O and P together teaches your right ring and pinky fingers to move independently while reaching from the home row to the top row.
