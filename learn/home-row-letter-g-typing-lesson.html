🖐️ Lesson: Type the Letter G

G is typed with your left index finger. Start with your finger resting on F,then move slightly upward and to the right to press G. Return to the home-row position after each keystroke.

How to Type the Letter G Correctly

The letter G is typed using your left index finger. From the normal home-row position,your index finger rests on F. To type G,move that finger slightly upward and to the right,press the key,and then return your hand to the home row.

This lesson uses four short drills — a single tap,double taps,triple taps,and a building rhythm — to help your left index finger learn the correct movement for G without looking down at the keyboard.

Which finger types the letter G?

Your left index finger. Begin with your index finger positioned on F,move slightly upward and right to G,then return to the home-row position.

Where is the G key on the keyboard?

The G key is on the upper letter row,between F and H. It is normally reached with the left index finger.
