🔢 Lesson: Type Numbers 6, 7, 8, 9 and 0

6 and 7 are typed with your right index finger, 8 with your right middle finger, 9 with your right ring finger, and 0 with your right pinky finger. These 4 exercises combine five number-row keys to build accurate typing muscle memory.

How to Type Numbers 6, 7, 8, 9 and 0 Correctly

The number 6 is typed with your right index finger, 7 with your right index finger, 8 with your right middle finger, 9 with your right ring finger, and 0 with your right pinky finger. Keep your right hand positioned on the home row and reach upward with the correct finger for each number.

These four progressive exercises help you move accurately across numbers 6 through 0 while developing number-row muscle memory. Focus on accuracy first and avoid looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type the numbers 6, 7, 8, 9 and 0?

Your right index finger types 6 and 7, right middle finger types 8, right ring finger types 9, and right pinky finger types 0. Return your fingers toward the home row after each keystroke.

How should I practice numbers 6, 7, 8, 9 and 0?

Keep your right hand relaxed on the home row. Reach upward for each number with the correct finger, focusing on clean and accurate keystrokes.
