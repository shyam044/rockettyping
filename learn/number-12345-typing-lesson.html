🔢 Lesson: Type Numbers 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5

1 is typed with your left pinky finger, 2 with your left ring finger, 3 with your left middle finger, and 4 and 5 with your left index finger. These 4 exercises combine five number-row keys to build accurate typing muscle memory.

How to Type Numbers 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 Correctly

The number 1 is typed with your left pinky finger, 2 with your left ring finger, 3 with your left middle finger, and 4 and 5 with your left index finger. Keep your left hand positioned on the home row and reach upward with the correct finger for each number.

These four progressive exercises help you move accurately across numbers 1 through 5 while developing number-row muscle memory. Focus on accuracy first and avoid looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type the numbers 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5?

Your left pinky types 1, left ring finger types 2, left middle finger types 3, and left index finger types 4 and 5. Return your fingers toward the home row after each keystroke.

How should I practice numbers 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5?

Keep your left hand relaxed on the home row. Reach upward for each number with the correct finger, focusing on clean and accurate keystrokes.
