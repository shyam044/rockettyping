🖐️ Practice Typing ER

E is typed with your left middle finger and R is typed with your left index finger. These two keys are next to each other on the top row. These exercises help you build coordination and muscle memory while typing E and R without looking at the keyboard.

How to Type E and R Correctly

The letters E and R are two important left-hand top-row keys used in touch typing. Press E with your left middle finger and R with your left index finger.

Keep your left hand relaxed and return your fingers to their home-row positions after each keystroke. Practice reaching upward to E and R without looking down at the keyboard.

Which fingers type E and R?

The letter E is typed with your left middle finger. The letter R is typed with your left index finger. Return both fingers to their home-row positions after each keystroke.

Why practice E and R together?

Practicing E and R together teaches your left middle and index fingers to move independently while reaching from the home row to the top row.
