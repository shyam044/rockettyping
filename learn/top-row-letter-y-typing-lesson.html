🖐️ Type the Letter Y

Y is typed with your right index finger. Reach up from the H or J area to press Y, then return your finger to its home-row position. These 4 exercises build the muscle memory you need to type Y accurately without looking at the keyboard.

How to Type the Letter Y Correctly

The letter Y is a top-row key typed with your right index finger. From the home row, your right index finger moves upward to Y and then returns to the home row. Keep your other fingers positioned on J, K, and L while practicing.

This lesson uses four short drills — a single tap, a double tap, a triple tap, and a building rhythm from one to five letters — so your right index finger learns the movement needed to reach Y accurately and consistently.

Which finger types the letter Y?

Your right index finger. Keep your hand on the home row, reach upward to Y, press the key, and return to the home row.

Is Y a home-row key?

No. Y is on the top row of a QWERTY keyboard. Your right index finger reaches upward to type it.
