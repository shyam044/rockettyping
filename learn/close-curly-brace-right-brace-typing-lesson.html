⌨️ Lesson: Type the Close Curly Brace (})

The close curly brace (}) is typed with Shift + [. Hold left Shift with your left pinky and tap the key with your right pinky. These 4 exercises take it from a single tap to real-world uses, with a live keyboard showing the exact fingers.

How to Type the Close Curly Brace (}) Correctly

The close curly brace — also called right curly bracket, right brace, closing brace — lives on the top row of a standard US keyboard. Hold the left Shift key with your left pinky, tap [ with your right pinky, then release both. It shares the ] key. Hold the left Shift, then reach with your right pinky.

The closing curly brace ends code blocks, objects and JSON data. Practising it now means you can type it without looking down when you meet it in emails, code, passwords or documents.

Note: keys can move on non-US layouts (UK, German, French and others), so if the symbol does not appear, check that your keyboard language is set to US English.

How do you type the close curly brace (}) on a keyboard?

Hold left Shift with your left pinky and press the [ key with your right pinky.

Which finger types the close curly brace (})?

Your right pinky types the [ key. The left pinky holds the left Shift key, so both hands share the work.

What is the close curly brace (}) used for?

The closing curly brace ends code blocks, objects and JSON data.
